Help & FAQ

Tap a question.

Where do I get games?

Use your own games: copy (dump) them from cartridges you own and add the .nds file with “Add a game”. CramBoy doesn’t offer or link to game downloads.

Which games work?

Most DS games play. Subtitles and word lookup work in these Pokémon games:

GameSubtitlesNames
HeartGold, SoulSilverKorean, Japanese
Black, WhiteKorean, Japanese
Diamond, Pearl, PlatinumKorean, Japanese–
Black 2, White 2Not tested yet–
Other DS gamesNone–

Subtitles need the Korean or Japanese version of the game; other versions play without them. Names: your name (and in HeartGold and SoulSilver your rival’s) is read from your save, so it isn’t underlined as an unknown word.

Why don’t I see subtitles?

Check that the game is in the list under “Which games work?” and is the Korean or Japanese version. Then check that Study mode is on: in a game, open Settings › Study.

How do I keep my save safe?

Save in the game as usual: CramBoy stores it a few seconds later. On iPhone, open CramBoy from your Home Screen, because Safari tabs can lose their data. For an extra copy, tap Backup at the top of the home screen, then Back up to a file: it keeps your saved words, progress and every game’s save in one file.

Can I move my save to another device?

Yes. Tap Backup at the top of the home screen, then Back up to a file; on the other device, use Backup › Restore from a file: your saved words, progress and game saves come along (add the games themselves again). For one game’s save, Export save and Import save in Settings › Game work too (.dsz, .sav and .dsv).

What do Learning and Known mean?

Tap a word in the subtitles: Learning saves it as a flashcard. It comes back in Review at growing intervals until you know it. Known removes the word’s underline in the subtitles, so you can focus on new words.

Why does it say “Update ready”?

A new version downloaded in the background. Save in the game, then tap Update ready in Settings › App. Your games, saves and words stay.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Your games, saves and words stay on this device. CramBoy only downloads the app itself and its dictionaries. The one exception is if you choose Backup › Back up to Google Drive: then a backup goes to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive, never to us.

How do I hide the controls or move the subtitle box?

The fingerprint button under the translate button hides the controls, so you can use the touch screen; the up arrow button brings them back. Drag the subtitle box by its top edge to move it, or by its corner to resize it. Reset box position in Settings › Study puts it back.
