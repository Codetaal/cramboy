Help & FAQ

Tap a question.

Where do I get games?

Use your own games: copy (dump) them from cartridges you own and add the .nds file with “Add a game”. CramBoy doesn’t offer or link to game downloads.

Why don’t I see subtitles?

Subtitles work in Pokémon HeartGold, SoulSilver, Black and White, in Korean and Japanese. In the game menu, check that Study mode is on.

How do I keep my save safe?

Save in the game as usual: CramBoy stores it a few seconds later. On iPhone, open CramBoy from your Home Screen, because Safari tabs can lose their data. For an extra copy, use Back up to a file on the home screen: it keeps your saved words, progress and every game’s save in one file.

Can I move my save to another device?

Yes. Back up to a file on the home screen, then use Restore from a file on the other device: your saved words, progress and game saves come along (add the games themselves again). For one game’s save, Export save and Import save in the game menu work too (.dsz, .sav and .dsv).

What do Learning and Known mean?

Learning saves a word as a flashcard. It comes back in Review at growing intervals until you know it. Known removes the word’s underline in the subtitles, so you can focus on new words.

Why does it say “Update ready”?

A new version downloaded in the background. Save in the game, then tap Update ready in the game menu. Your games, saves and words stay.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Your games, saves and words stay on this device. CramBoy only downloads the app itself and its dictionaries.

How do I hide the controls or move the subtitle box?

The fingerprint button under the translate button hides the controls, so you can use the touch screen; the arrow button brings them back. Drag the subtitle box by its top edge to move it, or by its corner to resize it. Reset box position in the game menu puts it back.
