Privacy

Last updated 20 November 2026

The short version

CramBoy runs entirely in your browser. Your games, game saves, saved words and progress stay on your device. CramBoy has no accounts, no analytics and no server that receives your data.

What stays on your device

The games you add, their saves, resume points, saved words, flashcard screenshots, reading progress and settings are stored in your browser’s own storage on this device. Removing CramBoy or clearing your browser data deletes them.

What CramBoy downloads

Only the app itself, its dictionaries and its subtitle fonts, from GitHub Pages (codetaal.github.io). GitHub may keep standard server logs of these requests, as it does for every website it hosts.

Backups

To a file: the file is made on your device and goes wherever you choose to save it.

To Google Drive (optional): if you choose this, you sign in with Google and allow CramBoy to use one permission, drive.appdata. It lets CramBoy create and read its own backup file in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive. CramBoy can’t see your other Drive files. The backup goes straight from your device to Google; it never passes through us. It contains your saved words, progress, settings and game saves, not the games. Disconnecting in CramBoy removes its access; you can also remove CramBoy at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which lets you delete its hidden data too. Google’s own privacy policy applies to your Google account.
